1 Abstract

baystability fits the linear-bilinear models of a multi-environment trial, AMMI and SREG (GGE), by the general Bayesian method of Perez-Elizalde et al. (2012). Its distinctive feature is that the data of an earlier trial can be the prior. This vignette analyses the second year of a maize trial with the first year as prior, from the data to the biplot with highest posterior density (HPD) regions and a written interpretation.

4 The Bayesian fit with the first year as prior

.prior_data makes the first year the prior. Its cell means, effects and singular value decomposition become the prior means, and its replicates set the prior’s weight. All 11 bilinear terms are fitted, as in the paper.

fm <-
  bayes_ammi(
      .data       = cultivo2009
    , .y          = y
    , .gen        = entry
    , .env        = site
    , .rep        = rep
    , .nIter      = 3000
    , .prior_data = cultivo2008
    , .n0         = NULL
    , .model      = "AMMI"
    , .k          = NULL
    , .burnin     = 1000
    , .thin       = 1
    , .chains     = 2
    , .priors     = bys_priors(
          mu_mean     = 0
        , mu_var      = 1e+8
        , tau_mean    = 0
        , tau_var     = 1e+8
        , delta_mean  = 0
        , delta_var   = 1e+8
        , lambda_mean = 0
        , lambda_var  = 1e+8
        , nu          = 1
        , s2          = NULL
        )
    , .level      = 0.95
    , .seed       = 2012
    , .align      = TRUE
    , .waic       = TRUE
    )

The posterior mean of the overall mean, 3848, lies between the two yearly means (4069 and 3627). The prior counts as much as the data, because both years have three replicates.

5 The biplot with HPD regions

bp <-
  bayes_ammi_biplot(
      fit           = fm
    , burnin        = 0
    , thin          = 1
    , pb            = c(0.10, 0.05)
    , plot_stable   = FALSE
    , plot_unstable = TRUE
    , ncolors       = 5
    , .which        = "both"
    , .grid         = 100
    )
bp$biplot

Only genotypes and environments whose HPD region excludes the origin are drawn with their regions: they interact significantly on the first two terms.

6 Interpretation

bys_interpret(
    x             = fm
  , higher_better = TRUE
  , top           = 5
  )
Bayesian AMMI analysis

11 bilinear term(s): lambda_1 = 3,665, lambda_2 = 3,282; the chains have
converged.

Findings:
- The chains have converged by the criteria of Crossa et al. (2011) (largest
  potential scale reduction factor 1.007); the smallest effective sample size
  of the main parameters is 1,040.
- 2 of 200 Heidelberger-Welch stationarity and half-width tests failed (about
  5% fail by chance at level 0.05).
- Overall mean 3,848 (95% HPD 3,789 to 3,910); error variance 1,705,000, a
  coefficient of variation of 33.9%.
- Genotypes significantly above average (HPD of the effect excludes zero): 10
  and 19; below average: 38, 40 and 42.
- Environments with significantly high means: 3, 5, 8, 18, 22 and 3 more;
  low: 2, 4, 7, 9, 10 and 8 more.
- Singular values with 95% HPD intervals: lambda_1 = 3,665 (2,886 to 4,436);
  lambda_2 = 3,282 (2,601 to 3,972); lambda_3 = 2,819 (2,210 to 3,437);
  lambda_4 = 2,500 (1,925 to 3,075); lambda_5 = 2,221 (1,665 to 2,853);
  lambda_6 = 1,852 (1,258 to 2,454); lambda_7 = 1,529 (944.7 to 2,105);
  lambda_8 = 1,233 (669.7 to 1,784); lambda_9 = 933.1 (398.3 to 1,496);
  lambda_10 = 642.7 (147.2 to 1,172); lambda_11 = 330.8 (0.2213 to 768.8).
- On the first bilinear term, genotypes with significantly positive scores:
  10, 20 and 42; negative: 2, 12 and 19.
- Environments with significantly positive first scores: 5 and 25; negative:
  4 and 9.
- Genotypes and environments with significant first scores of the same sign
  interact positively: the genotype yields more there than its main effect
  predicts; of opposite sign, less.

Recommendations:
- Recommend the genotypes with significant first scores for the environments
  whose scores have the same sign (bayes_ammi_biplot() shows the regions).
- Draw bayes_ammi_biplot() to see which genotypes and environments form
  homogeneous groups (overlapping HPD regions).

Cautions:
- The HPD interval of lambda_11 reaches down to near zero: the term is weakly
  supported and its scores are uncertain.
